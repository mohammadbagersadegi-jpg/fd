# 05 - Stage Design: RAIN DISTRICT, block 07

## Layout

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| World width | 1800 px |
| Visible frame | 480x270 |
| Walkable lane (depth) | z = 204 .. 250 (46 px) |
| Player start | x 80, z 226 |
| Finish trigger | x 1740 |
| Camera | follows the player with a 22 px look-ahead, clamped to `worldWidth - 480` |
| Camera lock | active during waves, restores on clear |

## Layers

| Layer | Size | Parallax | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| sky | 480x270 | 0.00 | indigo gradient, haze, small moon, stars |
| far | 512x270 | 0.15 | tileable skyline silhouettes with rooftop antennas |
| mid | 960x270 | 0.45 | tileable facades: window grid, shutters, awnings, 6 neon signs, pipes, AC units |
| near | 1800x270 | 1.00 | unique street: road, curb, slab sidewalk, puddles with reflected neon, lamps, bollards, bus stop |
| fg | 480x270 | 1.18 | overhead cables, paper lanterns, low bottom railing |

Rain, splashes, lightning (every 15-35 s with a short flash) and a vignette are
rendered by the engine, not baked into the layers.

## Waves

Spawn offsets are measured from the camera edge, so enemies always walk in from
off-screen regardless of the camera position.

| Wave | Trigger x | Enemies | Composition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 300 | 2 | rivet, rivet |
| 2 | 930 | 3 | rivet, **rivet_elite**, rivet |
| 3 | 1460 | 3 | **rivet_elite**, rivet, rivet |

Clearing a wave awards 500 points, unlocks the camera and advances the stage.
Touching x 1740 after the last wave ends the stage (`STAGE CLEAR` -> results).

## Props and pickups

| Prop | Position | HP | Drop |
|---|---|---|---|
| crate_a | 560, 240 | 2 | +24 HP |
| trash_a | 1080, 214 | 1 | +150 pts |
| crate_b | 1260, 246 | 2 | +24 HP |
| barrel_a | 1620, 218 | 2 | +24 HP |

Pickups blink during their last 2 seconds and are collected by proximity
(< 16 px horizontally, < 12 px in depth).

## Readability rules

* The fighting lane is the bottom third; background signage never crosses it.
* Foreground elements are overhead cables and a 8 px railing at the very bottom,
  never in front of a fighter's body.
* Neon reflections are drawn inside puddles only, so they cannot be confused
  with hit effects.
* Electric effects use the character's cyan, damage numbers use white/cyan for
  the player's hits and red for incoming damage.

## 8. Campaign layer (stages 2-8)

Stage 1 is hand-authored in `config/stage.json`. The rest of the campaign is
generated by `tools/stage_pipeline/make_stages.py` into `config/stages.json`
and checked into the repository, so the numbers can be reviewed in a diff.
The generator is deterministic: `make_stages.py --check` fails if the checked-in
file drifts, and `npm test` runs that check.

| # | Title | World px | Waves | Enemies | Timer | HP x | DMG x |
|---|-------|----------|-------|---------|-------|------|-------|
| 1 | RAIN DISTRICT | 1800 | 3 | 8 | 99 s | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| 2 | SOUTH DOCKS | 1900 | 3 | 9 | 104 s | 1.10 | 1.06 |
| 3 | NEON MARKET | 2000 | 3 | 9 | 109 s | 1.20 | 1.12 |
| 4 | STEEL YARD | 2100 | 4 | 12 | 114 s | 1.30 | 1.18 |
| 5 | SKYLINE TRANSIT | 2200 | 4 | 15 | 119 s | 1.40 | 1.24 |
| 6 | RED SECTOR | 2300 | 4 | 16 | 124 s | 1.50 | 1.30 |
| 7 | TOWER BASE | 2400 | 4 | 16 | 129 s | 1.60 | 1.36 |
| 8 | MR. X TOWER | 2500 | 4 | 16 | 134 s | 1.70 | 1.42 |

* Wave triggers are spread over `worldWidth - 520` and always increase, so the
  camera never has to scroll backwards to reach a wave.
* Every wave locks the camera. The last wave of stages 2-8 is a boss wave:
  one `overlord` (180 HP base, 1.25x draw scale, 340 px aggro) plus two escorts,
  announced by the `BOSS` banner.
* Props: 5 per generated stage (stage 1 keeps its hand-placed 4), alternating
  crate / trash / barrel / crate; every third one drops health, the rest score.
* Difficulty scales twice: `enemyHealthMul` (1.00 -> 1.70) is applied at spawn,
  `enemyDamageMul` (1.00 -> 1.42) is baked into each enemy's `attackDamage`, so
  later stages hit harder without touching the shared combat config.
* Stage titles/themes are a colour wash over the same five parallax layers.
  This is a deliberate scope limit, recorded in `README.md` section 13.

Hand-off: `onStageClear()` fills `results` and sets `stageClearNext`; after the
260-tick clear hold the game calls `startStage(stageIndex + 1)`, which runs
`setStage()` and re-initialises the stage, camera, props and player HP is kept.
The HUD shows `STAGE n / 8`; the results screen only appears after stage 8 and
lists stages cleared, score, hits and damage taken for the whole campaign.
